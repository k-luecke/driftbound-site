Independent research across disciplines.

Finding where a known result in one field answers an open question in another.

Driftbound Labs is an independent research effort that reads across disciplines at scale. The premise is that many hard problems wait less on a new discovery than on a connection — a result already well established in one field that turns out to answer an open question in another. The work is to find those pairings and join existing results into something new at the seam.

Each candidate connection is tested the same way. A capability is broken into properties shown somewhere but never together; the frame names the constraint that is really a design choice and the constraint that is a law or measurement limit; and the confirm, refute, and inconclusive rules are fixed before any data is examined. A negative result is meant to be as informative as a positive one.
