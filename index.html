Independent research across disciplines.

Finding where a result already established in one place answers an open question in another.

Driftbound Labs is an independent research effort. Many hard problems wait less on a new discovery than on a connection — a result already known in one place that answers an open question in another. The gap can be between fields, or between specialties within a single field. The lab builds a pipeline to find those gaps and carry each one through to a probable solution, an experimental method, and a result.

Every candidate is framed the same way: the constraint that is really a design choice separated from the one that is a law or measurement limit, with the confirm, refute, and inconclusive rules fixed before any data is examined. A negative result counts as much as a positive one.
