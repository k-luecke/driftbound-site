Privacy Policy

Effective date

September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Driftbound Labs LLC ("Driftbound Labs," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this website, communications with us, and products or services we may provide.

Information we may collect

We may receive information that you provide directly, such as your name, email address, organization, and the contents of messages you send to us.

Our website and hosting providers may also process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and request logs for security, reliability, and operational purposes.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, provide and improve our services, operate and secure our systems, maintain business records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

Research and data products

Driftbound Labs develops research and data products using public records, openly available information, licensed data, and other sources we are permitted to use. These products are intended to focus on organizations, infrastructure, projects, markets, and other business or public-interest information rather than private personal activity.

Where business contact information or information relating to identifiable individuals appears in source material, we seek to retain only what is relevant to legitimate research, verification, provenance, or business-intelligence purposes and to preserve the source context of that information.

Service providers

We may use third-party providers for website hosting, source-code hosting, communications, analytics, cloud infrastructure, data processing, and similar operational services. Those providers may process information on our behalf subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including research reproducibility, contractual obligations, security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided to us, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. Certain records may be retained when required for legal, security, contractual, archival, or research-integrity reasons.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services and practices evolve. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to admin@driftboundlabs.com.
